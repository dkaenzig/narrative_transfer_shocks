# Narrative Transfer Shocks

This repository provides the narrative transfer shock series constructed in

Guerreiro, Joao, Jonathon Hazell, Diego R. Känzig, and Ed Manuel (2026).  
*The Macroeconomic Effect of Stimulus Checks: Evidence from Postwar Veterans' Payments.*  
Working paper, September 2026.  
https://dkaenzig.github.io/diegokaenzig.com/Papers/ghkm_stimuluschecks.pdf

## Description

The dataset contains a narrative series of plausibly exogenous temporary transfer payments in the United States, constructed from a detailed historical analysis of postwar veterans' payments and temporary Social Security payments.

Following the narrative identification approach, transfer payments are classified according to their underlying motivation and timing. Payments unrelated to contemporaneous macroeconomic conditions are classified as plausibly exogenous transfer shocks. The resulting series records the months in which these payments were made.

The series can be used to study the macroeconomic effects of temporary transfer payments.

For further details on the construction of the series and the classification of events, see the paper.
